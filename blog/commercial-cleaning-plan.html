What a commercial cleaning plan includes

October 15, 2026

A commercial cleaning plan is a scope and a schedule for one building. It is not a package pulled off a shelf. Offices, clinics, shops, and restaurants do not need the same list, and a plan that ignores that will miss the rooms people actually notice.

Start with the building

The first step is a walkthrough. We look at the washrooms, entrances, lunchrooms, work areas, and the surfaces people touch all day. Those usually come before anything decorative. Carpet extraction, floor stripping, window cleaning, and pressure washing are separate visits when the building needs them. They are not automatically part of a nightly or weekly clean.

We clean offices, retail, medical and dental clinics, and other commercial facilities in Winnipeg, Selkirk, Oakbank, and Beausejour.

Put the work on a schedule

Say whether the building needs a cleaner daily, weekly, or monthly, and whether that visit happens during the day or after people have gone home. Office cleaning is often done during the day. The plan should name the days and what gets done on each one, so a missed washroom is obvious and a once-a-month floor is not expected every Tuesday.

Decide who brings the supplies

Except for a mop, broom, and vacuum, our crew brings its own green products and equipment. If you want a specific disinfectant, or you want the floors done with your own tools, leave them out and we will use them.

Leave room to change it

There is no long-term lock-in. Service can be paused or stopped. We also check the building during the contract, not only on the first day, so the standard you agreed to is the standard that stays.
