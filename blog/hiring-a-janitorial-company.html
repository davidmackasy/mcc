Questions to ask before you hire a janitorial company

October 8, 2026

Most quotes look similar on paper. The difference shows up after the company has a key to the building. These are the questions worth asking before you sign.

Are they bonded and insured, and are the cleaners checked?

Ask for both. Master Commercial Cleaning is bonded and insured, and has been cleaning commercial facilities since 2015. Every cleaner is background-checked and trained on that site's security rules before being sent in.

Do they work in your city?

We serve Winnipeg, Selkirk, Oakbank, Beausejour, and part of Manitoba. If the building is outside that area, ask before you assume a company will take it. A yes over the phone is not the same as a crew that already drives that route.

What is in the regular visit, and what is extra?

Daily upkeep is not the same job as carpet cleaning, floor waxing, interior and exterior glass, post-construction cleanup, or pressure washing. A clear quote names which of those are included and which are booked on their own. If the quote only says "full service," ask for the list.

Who do you call when something is missed?

You want a phone number and an email a person answers. Ours are +1 (204) 698-1133 and clean@mastercleaning.ca. The office is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, at Unit 7, 1599 Dugald Rd, Winnipeg.

Can you change the schedule later?

Buildings change. A company that requires a long contract for a simple weekly clean is a poor fit when you need to pause for a renovation or add a day. Our service can be paused or stopped at any time.

If you want those answers in writing for your own building, request an estimate and we will scope it from a walkthrough, not a template.
